Market intelligence · 2025 · live at pseye.site

A market intelligence platform for the Philippine Stock Exchange, covering all 282 listed companies. It has a market map, portfolio and watchlist tracking, dividend history, and foreign fund flow. Acoustify

Spotify Web API · 2023

A full-stack app on top of the Spotify Web API. A Node server handles the OAuth exchange, and the front end pulls your library, your most played songs and artists, and builds a page per song out of Spotify’s audio analysis: key, mode, tempo, and the acousticness and danceability scores. Flood Modeling & Visualization System

Hydrology · Simulation · 2024

My undergraduate thesis, funded under an NCTS research initiative. It models flood progression across Philippine river systems using the shallow water equations with RK2 integration over a cellular automaton grid, accounting for infiltration and critical flow thresholds. The inner loop is compiled with Numba, which is what made running several terrain scenarios practical. The output is a set of rainfall and flow thresholds usable for disaster risk planning.

PythonNumPyNumbaQGISMatplotlib
Code ↗ Paper ↗

Bank Fraud Risk Scoring

Finance · Fraud detection · 2024

A composite fraud risk score built over the NeurIPS 2022 bank account applications dataset, a little over a million rows. I worked through the feature distributions and per-feature fraud rates to find the ones carrying real signal, scored and bucketed applicants into risk tiers, and put the result behind a Power BI dashboard a reviewer can filter. The top tier lands at a 6.56% fraud rate, roughly six times the base rate across the portfolio.

PythonPandasPower BI

Perception Mapping of HIV Awareness in the Philippines

Public health · EDA · 2023

An analysis of the 2022 National Demographic and Health Survey, covering just over 35,000 households, on what people actually know about HIV. I built a composite perception score out of the individual knowledge questions so regions could be compared on one number, then broke the results down by region, age band, and education level.

PythonPandasPlotlyJupyter
