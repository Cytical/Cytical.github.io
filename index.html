Flood Modeling & Visualization System

Hydrology · Simulation · 2024

NCTS-funded thesis

A geospatial flood simulation built as my undergraduate thesis under an NCTS-funded research initiative. Applies shallow water equations and RK2 numerical integration with cellular automata to model flood progression, infiltration rates, and critical flow thresholds across Philippine river systems, optimized with Numba JIT. Derived flood risk thresholds from rainfall and flow analysis for disaster risk assessment.

ImpactFunded undergraduate research, and the thresholds it produced are usable for real disaster risk planning.

Bank Fraud Risk Scoring

Finance · Fraud detection · 2024

6.56% top-tier fraud rate

A composite fraud risk score engineered over 1M+ bank account applications from the NeurIPS 2022 dataset. I analyzed feature distributions and fraud rates to isolate high-signal predictors, segmented applicants into risk tiers, and surfaced the result in an interactive Power BI dashboard. The top tier carries a 6.56% fraud rate — roughly 6× the portfolio average.

ImpactTurned 1M+ raw applications into a tiered screening rule a reviewer can act on.
