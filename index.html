PSEye

live since 2026, updated every trading day

PSEye is a market intelligence platform for the Philippine Stock Exchange. It covers all 282 listed companies, and it is the project I have put the most work into. Everything below is running in production right now, and it gets around 2,500 visitors a month.

What is on it

Market map ↗
A treemap of the whole exchange, sized by market cap and coloured by the day's move. It filters down to the PSEi 30, the top 50 or 100, or your own watchlist, and drills into any of the six sectors.
Company pages ↗
Price history, disclosures and news per ticker, plus a statistical profile: annualised return, Sharpe and Sortino, downside deviation, the daily return distribution with its skew and excess kurtosis, and 95% VaR and CVaR.
Daily recap ↗
A page per trading day, written from the close: index level and trend, total market cap against its one-year range, sector movers, and the day's gainers, losers and most active names.
Foreign fund flow ↗
Daily net foreign buying and selling parsed out of the PSE's Daily Quotation Report, plus weekly gross buying against selling from the Market Watch report, which is the only source that separates the two sides.
Market regime ↗
Risk-on and risk-off labelling over a reconstructed composite index, from three signals: trend against the 200-day average, drawdown from the peak, and 30-day volatility. It describes where the market has been, it does not forecast.
Explorer and screener ↗
Every tracked company in one sortable table: price, change, market cap both raw and PSE-adjusted, float, trailing twelve-month dividend yield, P/E and P/B. There is a dividend screener too, with ex-dates and payout history.

How it is built

The hard part was never the charts. PSE data arrives in inconsistent shapes across several sources, so most of the work went into the ingestion pipeline and the schema that pulls all of it into one queryable model. Once that existed, the daily recap, the fund flow page and the regime labelling all fell out of the same tables instead of each needing its own scraper.

Prices and disclosures are ingested daily, and the recap is generated from the close rather than written by hand. That is the only reason a page per trading day is maintainable at all.

Flood Modeling & Visualization System

Hydrology · Simulation · 2024

My undergraduate thesis, funded under an NCTS research initiative. It models flood progression across Philippine river systems using the shallow water equations with RK2 integration over a cellular automaton grid, accounting for infiltration and critical flow thresholds. The inner loop is compiled with Numba, which is what made running several terrain scenarios practical. The output is a set of rainfall and flow thresholds usable for disaster risk planning.

PythonNumPyNumbaQGISMatplotlib

Bank Fraud Risk Scoring

Finance · Fraud detection · 2024

A composite fraud risk score built over the NeurIPS 2022 bank account applications dataset, a little over a million rows. I worked through the feature distributions and per-feature fraud rates to find the ones carrying real signal, scored and bucketed applicants into risk tiers, and put the result behind a Power BI dashboard a reviewer can filter. The top tier lands at a 6.56% fraud rate, roughly six times the base rate across the portfolio.

PythonPandasPower BI
